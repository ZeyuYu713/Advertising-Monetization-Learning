# 案例08｜International Ads Product：英文业务面试训练

这一节是业务表达练习，不应把例子说成自己经历。实际面试中先回答核心判断，再解释原因和验证。

## Q1. What is the difference between brand and performance advertising?

**Answer:** Brand advertising focuses on outcomes such as awareness, ad recall and consideration, while performance advertising is generally optimized toward measurable actions such as leads, app installs or purchases. The distinction is mainly about the advertiser's objective, not simply the ad format. A video in the feed may support either goal.

## Q2. How would you diagnose a 20% decline in brand ad revenue?

**Answer:** I would first verify the financial and reporting definitions. Then I would break down revenue into delivered impressions and realized revenue per thousand impressions, segmenting by market, format, buying type and advertiser cohort. After identifying whether the issue is advertiser demand, available inventory, delivery or product mix, I would propose a measurable product solution and test its incremental impact.

## Q3. Why might a campaign fail to spend its budget?

**Answer:** It depends on the buying type. For auction campaigns, I would inspect campaign eligibility, targeting constraints, bidding and cost controls, creative quality, competition and conversion feedback. For reserved campaigns, I would examine the booking terms, forecast inventory, audience overlap, frequency constraints and delivery reconciliation.

## Q4. What are Reach and Frequency?

**Answer:** Reach is the number of unique people exposed to an ad within a specified time period, while impressions count exposures, including repeated exposures. Average frequency is impressions divided by reach. I would also examine the frequency distribution rather than relying only on the average.

## Q5. What is a Creative?

**Answer:** A creative is the advertising message and how it is communicated to users. Depending on the product and reporting context, an ad creative may also refer to a specific configurable or reportable object, such as a video-based execution. It is different from the advertising format and the underlying asset file.

## Q6. How would you evaluate a new ad format?

**Answer:** I would identify the advertiser problem and the user context, define inventory and buying constraints, design the creative and measurement workflow, and estimate net incremental revenue after accounting for cannibalization. I would then run an appropriately designed experiment with advertiser value, user experience and brand safety guardrails.

## Q7. What is the difference between attribution and incrementality?

**Answer:** Attribution credits conversions to touchpoints under a chosen rule. Incrementality estimates the additional outcomes caused by advertising compared with a credible counterfactual. Attributed conversions are not necessarily incremental conversions.

## Q8. What if your A/B experiment is not statistically significant?

**Answer:** I would look at the effect estimate, confidence interval, statistical power, sample quality and experiment design. A non-significant result does not prove the two options are identical. I would decide whether to gather more evidence, revise the hypothesis or stop based on business value and guardrails.

## Q9. How do you prioritize requests from different international sales teams?

**Answer:** I would evaluate the underlying problem, affected customers and markets, business impact, urgency, compliance requirements, confidence in the evidence and development effort. I would distinguish a reusable product capability from a one-off service request.

## Q10. How do you collaborate with engineers and data scientists?

**Answer:** I would translate business problems into decision points, measurable objectives, clear requirements and acceptance criteria. I would align on data quality, technical constraints, ownership and rollout risks, then use experiments and user feedback to validate the solution.

## 适合主动补充的英文术语

Advertiser demand、Inventory forecasting、Campaign delivery、Ad format、Buying type、Creative assets、Reach and Frequency、Brand Lift、Ad Recall、Incremental revenue、Guardrail metrics、Experiment design、Sample ratio mismatch、Revenue cannibalization、Cross-functional alignment。
